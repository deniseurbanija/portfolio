Taller App

Tire shops still run on paper checklists. This one doesn't anymore.

I built a workflow app around how the shop actually operates, and today 8 staff members across 2 branches use it every day.

reception

Finds the car by license plate, so returning customers come pre-filled. Logs mileage, notes and the services it needs.

workshop

Rates each tire on exit with a traffic-light check (good, fair, replace) and marks every service as done.

customer

Gets the PDF work order on WhatsApp and by email, the moment the car is ready.

Each branch sees only its own queue and history. The result: less paper, cleaner service records and a faster handoff between the front desk and the workshop floor.
